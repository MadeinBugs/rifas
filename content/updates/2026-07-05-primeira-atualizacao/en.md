## How It Works

Every illness has its own protocol. In Suspiro's case, the treatment consists
of *6 cycles of 3 chemotherapy sessions*, with a one-week break between cycles.

We are currently on the **3rd cycle**, **2nd session**.

Last week he started eating on his own for the first time in a long while —
he had been tube-fed for so long — but unfortunately this week he fell ill
again and stopped wanting to eat.

![Suspiro refusing food](/photos/atualizacao/suspiro-comida.jpeg)

We did an X-ray that showed the mass is no longer visible. Right now,
**Suspiro shows no clinical signs — or signs in any exam — of the disease!**
This is the best prognosis we could have hoped for, and we are incredibly,
incredibly lucky. &#128150;

Even so, we need to finish the full cycle to make sure the lymphoma doesn't
come back.

![X-ray](/photos/atualizacao/raio-x.png)

Even with this good news, the fight is daily — chemotherapy takes a heavy toll.
His healthy weight was **5.600 kg** and he is now down to **2.900 kg**.

He lost so much weight that he can no longer keep himself warm on his own,
so he even needs a heater now.

![Skinny Suspiro next to his heater](/photos/atualizacao/aquecedor.jpeg)
